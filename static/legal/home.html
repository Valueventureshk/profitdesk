What it does

From "how are we doing?" to a decision in one place

True profit dashboard

Sales, refunds, chargebacks, payment fees, product cost and ad spend for every store and day, as a profit-and-loss waterfall. Every figure has one definition.

Server-side tracking

A first-party Shopify pixel records adds to cart, checkouts and purchases per campaign, ad set and ad, and sends conversions to Google Ads and Meta.

Google Ads product desk

Google Shopping and Performance Max spend for every product next to its real sales and cost, with verdicts to scale, watch or cut.

Ads assistant

An AI assistant that proposes changes to your own campaigns (new campaigns, moving winners, budgets) and only acts when you approve, with undo.

Cash flow & supplier accounts

What's in the bank, what's held by payment processors and when it arrives, and what you owe each supplier.

Ask it anything

Chat with your numbers in the app or on WhatsApp: "how did Beige Rue do yesterday?", "which products lost money this week?".

Our story

Built by a store owner, not a software company

NM
"I was paying for a profit app, a tracking app, a parcel tracker, a spy tool and three spreadsheets, and I still couldn't answer one question: did we make money yesterday?"

Nafras Mohamed runs a group of Shopify stores across Australia, North America and Europe through Value Ventures Limited, spending on Meta and Google Ads every day and shipping through overseas suppliers.

After years of stitching together apps that each saw only part of the picture, he built his own: one system that reads the stores, the ad accounts, the payment processors and the supplier invoices, and reconciles them to the cent. It now runs his whole operation every day: profit, tracking, ads, cash and customer support.

EcomVital is that system, being prepared for other brands who are tired of guessing.
