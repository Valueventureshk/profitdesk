ProfitDesk privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026. Operated by Value Ventures Limited.

ProfitDesk is an internal reporting tool used only by Value Ventures Limited and its business partner to see sales, advertising spend and profit for our own online stores. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What data ProfitDesk reads

  • Shopify: order dates, totals, refunds and how each order was paid, for our own stores, read-only.
  • Google: with our permission, read-only access to our own Google Ads spend and to a Google Sheet we own that contains that spend. ProfitDesk does not read emails, contacts or any other Google data, and never changes our Google Ads accounts.
  • Meta: read-only ad spend for our own ad accounts.

How it is used and stored

Data is used only to calculate sales, ad spend, fees, ROAS and profit on our private dashboard. It is stored on our own server, accessible only to logged-in members of our business. We do not sell, share, or use the data for advertising, and we do not transfer it to third parties.

ProfitDesk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions), Shopify admin (Settings → Apps) or Meta Business Settings, and data is deleted from ProfitDesk when the connection is removed.
