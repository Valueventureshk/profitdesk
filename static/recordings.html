Session Recordings

Watch what shoppers did on your stores. What they type is never recorded.

How it works, and how to switch it on

What it does

When it's on for a store, a small script records what shoppers see and do on the store: pages, scrolling, taps and clicks, menus they open. You watch it here like a video. It isn't a screen video, so it's light and doesn't slow the store down.

What it never records

  • Anything typed into a box (names, emails, addresses, search, discount codes): it shows as ••••.
  • The checkout and the customer account pages (no payment details, ever).
  • Shoppers who said no to analytics in the store's cookie banner.

Switch it on for a store

  1. Below, under Recording on these stores, open the list and tick Record next to the store. (Needs Manage access to this app.)
  2. Choose the share of visitors to record (start with 20–30%), the max a day (recordings stop for the day after this many) and how many days to keep them.
  3. Within a minute, new visitors start being recorded. Each session appears in the list a few seconds after the shopper starts browsing; click ▶ Watch.
  4. Untick Record to stop: the script is removed from the store straight away.

Before you switch it on

  • The store needs the App proxy (the same one the order tracking page uses). Amber Ashton doesn't have it yet.
  • Add a line about session recording to the store's privacy policy, for example: “We record anonymised browsing sessions (pages viewed, clicks, scrolling) to improve the store. We never record what you type or any payment details.” EU, UK and Canadian stores especially.

Using it well

  • Filter Opened the cart to watch shoppers who nearly bought: where did they stop?
  • Filter Phones: most of your traffic. Look for things they tap that don't work, or text they can't read.
  • Recordings older than the keep days are deleted automatically, oldest first if storage fills up.
